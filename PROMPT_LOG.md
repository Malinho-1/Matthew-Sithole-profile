# Prompt Log — About Me Section

## 1. My Prompt

> Write a short 2-3 sentence 'About Me' paragraph for a student portfolio
> page. I'm a CIS student at Africa University studying web tech. Keep it
> under 40 words, first person, no corporate buzzwords.

## 2. Raw AI Output

> I'm Matthew, a Computer Information Systems student at Africa University
> with a focus on web technologies. I enjoy building practical, real-world
> applications and I'm always looking to sharpen my skills in new tools and
> languages. Currently exploring Git, mobile app development, and
> problem-solving through code.

## 3. My Final Edited Version

> I'm Matthew, a Computer Information Systems student at Africa University
> focused on web technologies. I enjoy building practical, real-world
> applications, and I'm currently exploring Git and mobile app development
> through hands-on projects.

## 4. Reflection

The AI's answer actually came out to 45 words, over the 40-word limit I gave
it in the prompt, so I had to trim it myself. I cut "always looking to
sharpen my skills in new tools and languages" since it was close to the kind
of vague buzzword phrasing I'd asked it to avoid. I also merged the last two
sentences into one so it reads more naturally and fits the word count.
